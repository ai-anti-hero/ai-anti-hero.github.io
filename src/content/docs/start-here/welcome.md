## What is AI Anti-Hero?

The AI world is dominated by two loud extremes:
1. **The Evangelists:** Selling magic wands, promised AGIs by next Tuesday, and claiming every workflow should be completely handed over to chatbots.
2. **The Doomers:** Convinced that either the machines are conscious and coming for our souls, or that the whole thing is completely useless snake oil.

**AI Anti-Hero** sits right in the back row. We build things that work, measure what actually fails, and reject unearned mystique.

<RealTalk title="The Guy in the Back">
When someone claims their agentic pipeline has "human-level reasoning," the AI Anti-Hero is the one asking to see the error rates, the retry costs, and the edge cases. Pragmatism beats hype every single time.
</RealTalk>

## How this Guide is Structured

- **Field Guide Dispatches:** Deep architectural and conceptual essays on what models actually do under the hood, starting with anthropomorphism and token probability.
- **Playbooks for Leadership:** How to evaluate vendor pitches, spot fake AI features, and direct capital toward practical ROI.
- **Playbooks for Engineers:** Hard-won patterns for evals, deterministic guardrails, structured outputs, and real production pipelines.
