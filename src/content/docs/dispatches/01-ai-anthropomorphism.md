### Key Themes:

<RealTalk title="Real Talk: Intent vs. Prediction">
A model doesn't "know" the answer, nor does it "lie" when it hallucinates. It computes the most statistically probable next tokens based on context weights. If you design systems expecting intent, your systems will break.
</RealTalk>
